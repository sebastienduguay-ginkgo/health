🛡️ Safety & Progression Guidelines
  • Prioritize form over load. Use a weight that allows perfect execution without hitting failure.
  • Protect the wrists by keeping them straight in the rack and overhead positions.
  • Brace the abs hard during all standing movements to shield the lower back.
  • Finish with 5 minutes of light stretching focused on the chest, shoulders, and lats.
  • If your form breaks on the last rep, reduce the load and keep the movement quality clean.
💡 Practical Notes

Start Light

Because kettlebell work is dynamic, it is better to begin with a manageable weight and build quality before adding load.

Alternate With Running

On your alternating days, follow the run plan while keeping the kettlebell session low-stress and well-controlled.

Consistency Wins

Three 25-minute sessions per week are enough to create real strength improvements when recovery is respected.

Joint Friendly

The floor press and supported rowing pattern provide a compact, efficient upper-body stimulus that is generally kinder to a 50+ body.
