💡 Programme Guidelines

🌡️ Heart Rate is Your Gauge

During run intervals stay between 140–162 bpm. If you regularly exceed 165, slow your pace — don't cut the interval. Speed comes later; aerobic base comes first.

🚶 Always Warm Up 5 Minutes

Walk briskly for 5 minutes before your first run interval. At 50, your tendons, joints and cardiovascular system need this ramp-up to prevent injury.

🐢 Keep Your Run Pace Easy

Your ideal pace is 6:30–6:55/km — the pace you already use. Don't speed up as intervals lengthen. Easy pace builds the aerobic base that makes long runs possible.

🔄 Never Skip Recovery Weeks

Weeks 4 and 8 are mandatory lighter weeks. Your body builds fitness during rest, not during the run. Skipping these is the #1 cause of injury and plateau.

😴 Sleep is the Secret Weapon

Aim for 7–8 hours. Growth hormone — which repairs muscle and tendons — is released during deep sleep. Poor sleep undoes good training at any age.

📱 Programming WorkOutdoor

Update your interval timer at the start of each week. Tap the interval, set Run time + Walk time + repetitions. Always prepend a 5-min walking warm-up segment.

📅 Missed a Session?

Do not double up to compensate. Just resume from the next planned session. One missed run has zero impact on a 12-week plan. Two weeks off matters; one session doesn't.

🦵 Manage Phase 3 Carefully

Weeks 9–12 involve long continuous running. If your knees or shins hurt (not just tired) during a long run, walk the rest. Don't power through pain — back off one week and retry.
